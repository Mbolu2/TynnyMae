Vendor Partnership

Vendor Terms & Conditions

These terms govern the relationship between Tynny-Mae Luxury (HomeSupply) and all registered vendors. By registering, you agree to these terms in full.

1. Eligibility & Registration
  • Be a registered business or verified individual supplier operating in Lagos State.
  • Supply food items, household essentials, or related FMCG products of verifiable quality.
  • Provide valid contact details, a business bank account, and agree to quality inspection on demand.
  • Not be on any government watchlist or have pending food safety violations.
2. Product Standards & Quality
  • Items must be fresh, unexpired, and within 70%+ of shelf life at time of delivery.
  • Packaging must be clean, intact, and appropriately sealed.
  • Quantities must match the assigned order exactly — no substitutions without admin approval.
  • NAFDAC registration required for processed food items.
  • Tynny-Mae reserves the right to conduct random quality checks at any time.
3. Order Fulfilment
  • Vendors receive their fulfilment list at least 48 hours before each pack-by deadline.
  • All items must be packed and ready by the confirmed pack-by time.
  • Shortfalls must be reported immediately via the vendor dashboard — not on dispatch day.
  • Below 85% fulfilment over 2 consecutive cycles triggers a performance review.
4. Pricing & Remittance
  • Pricing agreed at onboarding, reviewed quarterly. No mid-cycle price changes.
  • Remittance within 3–7 business days of confirmed delivery.
  • Platform fee of 8–12% per fulfilled order (confirmed at onboarding).
  • All remittances to the registered bank account. Notify admin in writing of any changes.
5. Performance & Conduct
  • Performance tracked by: fulfilment rate, on-time rate, shortfall rate, complaint count.
  • 90%+ fulfilment with zero complaints qualifies for Featured Vendor status.
  • Vendors must not contact subscribers directly. All communication through Tynny-Mae admin.
  • Subscriber data is strictly confidential.
6. Termination

Immediate suspension for: expired/adulterated goods, confidentiality breach, fraudulent quantities, or 3+ fulfilment failures in one quarter. Terminated vendors may reapply after 90 days.

By registering, you confirm full agreement. Tynny-Mae may update terms with 14 days' notice to active vendors.
