Vendor Partnership

Vendor Terms & Conditions

These terms govern the relationship between Tynny-Mae Luxury (HomeSupply) and all registered vendors on the marketplace platform. By registering, you agree to these terms in full.

1. Eligibility & Registration

To register as a Tynny-Mae HomeSupply vendor, you must:

  • Be a registered business or verified individual supplier operating in Lagos State.
  • Supply food items, household essentials, or related FMCG products of verifiable quality.
  • Provide valid contact details, a bank account in your business name, and agree to quality inspection on demand.
  • Not be on any government watchlist or have pending food safety violations.
2. Product Standards & Quality

All products supplied through Tynny-Mae HomeSupply must meet the following standards:

  • Items must be fresh, unexpired, and within 70% or more of their shelf life at time of delivery.
  • Packaging must be clean, intact, and appropriately sealed.
  • Quantities must match the assigned order exactly — no substitutions without admin approval.
  • NAFDAC registration is required for processed food items.
  • Tynny-Mae reserves the right to conduct random quality checks at any time.
3. Order Fulfilment
  • Vendors receive their fulfilment list at least 48 hours before each cycle's pack-by deadline.
  • All items must be packed and ready for dispatch by the confirmed pack-by time.
  • Shortfalls must be reported to admin immediately via the vendor dashboard — not on the day of dispatch.
  • Repeated shortfalls (below 85% fulfilment rate over 2 consecutive cycles) will result in review and potential suspension.
  • Vendors are responsible for proper labelling and temperature-appropriate packaging where applicable.
4. Pricing & Remittance
  • Vendor pricing is agreed at onboarding and reviewed quarterly. Mid-cycle price changes are not permitted.
  • Tynny-Mae will remit payment to vendors within 3–7 business days of confirmed delivery, as agreed at onboarding.
  • A platform fee of 8–12% applies on each fulfilled order (exact rate confirmed at onboarding).
  • All remittances are made to the bank account provided at registration. Vendors must notify admin in writing of any changes.
  • Disputed remittances must be raised within 5 business days of the remittance date.
5. Performance & Growth
  • Vendor performance is tracked by: fulfilment rate, on-time rate, shortfall rate, and subscriber complaint count.
  • High-performing vendors (90%+ fulfilment, zero complaints) are eligible for Featured Vendor status and priority bundle assignment.
  • Vendors with consistent issues will be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan before any suspension decision.
6. Conduct & Confidentiality
  • Vendors must not contact subscribers directly. All communication is through the Tynny-Mae admin team.
  • Subscriber data, order data, and pricing information are strictly confidential and must not be shared or used outside of order fulfilment.
  • Vendors may not list the same products on competing subscription provisioning platforms without disclosure.
7. Termination

Tynny-Mae may suspend or terminate a vendor account immediately for:

  • Supplying expired, adulterated, or falsely described goods.
  • Breach of confidentiality or subscriber contact terms.
  • Fraudulent representation of quantities or quality.
  • Three or more fulfilment failures in a single quarter.

A terminated vendor may re-apply after 90 days with evidence of corrective action.

By registering as a vendor, you confirm you have read, understood, and agreed to these terms. Tynny-Mae Luxury reserves the right to update these terms with 14 days' notice to active vendors.
