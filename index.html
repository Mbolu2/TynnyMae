The Process

Five steps to a provisioned home

01
Select Your Plan
Browse our three curated HomeSupply plans — Basic, Family, or Plus. Each is designed for a specific household size and provisioning need. View full bundle contents before committing.
02
Complete Prepayment
Pay upfront via card or bank transfer. Your subscription is confirmed only upon payment, protecting both you and our vendors from uncertainty.
03
Receive Your Schedule
Immediately after confirmation, you receive your delivery calendar for the full cycle. Know exactly when your bundle arrives — no guessing, no waiting for updates.
04
We Fulfil Your Bundle
Our verified vendor network sources, packs, and dispatches your bundle. Every item is quality-checked before leaving our fulfilment partners. You track status in real time.
05
Renew With One Click
Seven days before expiry, we remind you. Your plan, address, and preferences are saved. Renewal takes one click. Your household never runs dry.
